Red Flags You May Have Overlooked in the Email:

  • Artificial Sense of Urgency: Demands immediate action within "24 hours" or threats of account suspension.
  • Generic or Mismatched Sender: The display name may look official, but the actual sender domain was fictitious.
  • Unverified Link Destination: The text said "Validate" or "Reschedule", but hovering over the button revealed an unexpected URL.
  • Unsolicited Request: Prompting you to verify system credentials or packages without prior notice.

What Should You Do in Real Life?

1
Stop & Think

Never rush into clicking links under artificial deadlines.

2
Hover First

Hover your cursor over the link to verify the true URL destination.

3
Report to IT

Forward suspicious emails directly to your IT security helpdesk.
