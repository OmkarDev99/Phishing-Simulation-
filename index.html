🏫 Project Architecture & Viva Voce Defense

Frontend (Publicly Hosted): Enables students and staff to access the awareness guide and test their phishing defense readiness from any web browser.
Backend Simulation (Localhost Only): GoPhish (port 3333) and MailHog (port 1025/8025) operate inside an isolated offline lab on Windows, ensuring 100% legal compliance and preventing simulated lures from reaching public networks.
